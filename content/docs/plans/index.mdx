## FAQ

**What counts as a request?**
Each MCP tool call (e.g. `outlook_search_emails`) counts as one request. A single Claude prompt may trigger multiple tool calls.

**When does my quota reset?**
Quotas reset on the 1st of each month.

**Can I upgrade or downgrade at any time?**
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period.

**How do I manage my subscription?**
Go to [Settings → Billing](https://conflu.xyz/dashboard/settings) in your dashboard.
