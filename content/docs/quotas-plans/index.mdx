# Quotas and plans

Conflu enforces **monthly** limits on MCP **requests** and **egress** (response bytes). Limits depend on your plan. Account limits are separate.

## What counts as one request

**One MCP `tools/call` = one request**, no matter how many mailboxes you query. Calling `microsoft_list_emails` with `account_id: "all"` and three connected accounts still consumes **one** request and increments egress by the size of the combined JSON response.

## Monthly period

Usage resets on the **first day of each calendar month at 00:00 UTC**. The dashboard shows when the current period ends. When a top-up is active, whatever you used beyond your plan's included quota is deducted from it at the reset; the rest carries over.

## Plan limits (defaults)

These are the default caps from Conflu's plan configuration. Your user document may override `requestsLimit` / `egressLimitBytes` after billing changes.

| Plan | Connected accounts | MCP requests / month | Egress / month |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 2 | 500 | 50 MB |
| Pro | 10 | 50,000 | 50 GB |
| Ultra | 25 | 250,000 | 100 GB |

Free and Pro include **package connectors** (Microsoft 365 + Google). Pro and Ultra unlock **premium connectors** when they ship. **Web Crawling** is Ultra-only.

Pro top-ups (+10,000 requests or +50 GB egress, $10 each) are planned but not
self-serve yet: contact support if you need extra capacity before your reset date.

## When you hit a limit

The MCP server returns a Conflu error before calling the provider:

- `QUOTA_EXCEEDED_REQUESTS` — monthly request cap (including top-ups) reached
- `QUOTA_EXCEEDED_EGRESS` — monthly egress cap reached

Upgrade your plan or wait for the UTC month rollover.

## Free-tier data shaping

On Free, email bodies and large attachments may be truncated or omitted compared to Pro/Ultra. Tool responses still count toward egress.

## See your usage

Dashboard → **Usage** shows requests and egress for the current period. Advanced mode adds per-day charts and a request log (tool name, latency, status, error code — not message content).
