# Conflu documentation

Conflu is multi-account MCP middleware. You add **one** endpoint to your AI client,
connect **many** accounts, and pick the mailbox per call with `account_id`. No
reconfiguration when you switch client.

## Start here

- **[Getting started](/docs/get-started)** — create an account, connect a mailbox, call
  your first tool.
- **[Connect your AI client](/docs/connect-ai-client)** — Claude Desktop, Claude Code,
  Cursor, ChatGPT, and anything else that speaks MCP over HTTP.
- **[Accounts](/docs/accounts)** — connecting, reconnecting, and what *primary* means.
